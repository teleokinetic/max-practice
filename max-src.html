Why we vary it

This is the broader motor learning context from Claude: "These recordings deliberately revisit the same territory — breath, pelvis, lower belly — in different positions, speeds, and durations. Coming back to the same place along different paths builds retention, and it carries into ordinary activity better than repeating one fixed sequence."

Start here. A short floor lesson on the spine as the channel between pelvis and head: the pelvic clock, a gentle nod, then the two together.
